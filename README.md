## 🧑‍🔬 About Me

- 🎓 **M.S. student in Computer Technology**, School of Artificial Intelligence, Shenzhen University.
- 🤖 Interested in **humanoid robotics, robot learning, and whole-body motion control**.
- 🧠 Exploring reinforcement learning, imitation learning, and physics-informed robot control.
- 🦾 Working on object interaction and load-adaptive control for humanoid robots.
- 🛠️ Building simulation-based experiments with Isaac Gym, Isaac Lab, and robot learning frameworks.
- 📚 Interested in reproducing research papers, designing rigorous experiments, and developing reproducible robotics systems.

My goal is to bridge the gap between **learning-based control, robot dynamics, and reliable real-world behavior**.
